    How We Met

    It started the way the best things do — quietly, and without either of us planning it. A hello that should have been forgettable, and instead became the thing everything else got measured against.

    We left in the dark. The toll lights went past one after another and you were right there beside me the whole way.

    We rolled into Surabaya past midnight and you were still laughing, still not ready to go home. Cake, chaos, and you.

    I remember thinking: I want a thousand more of these.

    Our favourite memory / inside joke

    At 12 AM you said “one more hour.” We stayed until the sky turned. We always stay.
